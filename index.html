Research

The dedicated thread for the question underneath most of my work: when a neural network is confident, should you be?

Explanation Consistency as a Coherence Prior for Reliable Neural Decision-Making

Extended position paper · submitted to IJCNN 2026 · in review

Deep networks can be highly accurate and still fail silently. This paper proposes explanation consistency — whether a model's explanations stay stable under small input perturbations — as a reliability signal orthogonal to predictive accuracy, evaluated on medical imaging datasets.

The core argument: a model can generalize from the wrong features and never be caught by accuracy metrics alone. Explanation stability surfaces exactly this gap.

finding: high-confidence predictions can have unstable explanations — exposing failure modes accuracy metrics never see. in safety-critical systems, that gap is the whole problem.
